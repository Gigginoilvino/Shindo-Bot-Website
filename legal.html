Privacy Policy

Document 2

Explains what data Spawn Alert Bot collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1.What we collect

The Bot does not request Discord's Message Content, Presence, or Server Members privileged intents, and does not read your chat messages. It stores only what its features need to function:

  • Discord IDs — your user ID (for subscriptions and admin checks) and server/channel IDs (for routing alerts);
  • Command inputs — the item you subscribed to, a submitted server code, a boss-drop report you post, or an event toggle you set;
  • Per-server configuration — alert channel, drop channel, local admin roles, event flags, and similar settings a server admin configures.

We don't collect your email, IP address, payment information, or anything beyond what Discord's own API exposes for the interactions above.

2.How it's used

Data is used strictly to run the features you or your server admin turned on: sending you a subscribed-item ping, posting alerts in the configured channel, showing private-server codes for your request, and recording your boss-drop reports for other users. We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any secondary purpose.

3.Sharing & Discord

We don't sell or share your data with third parties. Because the Bot runs on Discord's platform, the data it processes is necessarily also visible to Discord Inc. under its own Privacy Policy. Discord's use of your data is governed by their terms, not ours.

4.Retention & deletion

Subscription and configuration data is kept for as long as you remain subscribed, or the server keeps the Bot installed. Removing the Bot from a server deletes that server's configuration on next sync (typically within 24 hours). Unsubscribing via /unsubscribe removes your subscription data immediately. Boss-drop reports and user-submitted codes are retained for as long as they remain useful to the community (typically 30+ days), then removed during data cleanup cycles.

5.Security

Data is stored in the Bot's own configuration files on infrastructure controlled by its operators. We take reasonable steps to keep this data restricted to the Bot's operators, but — as with any online service — no system is 100% secure. We do not encrypt data at rest, but access is restricted to developers via authentication keys.

6.Your rights

You can view your own subscriptions with /mysubs, remove them with /unsubscribe, and request a copy or deletion of any data tied to your Discord ID by contacting leon.nucera@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days. You also have the right to opt out of the Bot's use entirely by removing it from servers you administer.

7.Children's privacy

The Bot follows Discord's own minimum age policy (currently 13+) and does not knowingly collect data from users below that age beyond what Discord's platform itself already requires for account creation. If we learn that a user is below the minimum age, we will remove their data upon request or as required by law.
